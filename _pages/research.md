## Research Focus

My research interests are centered on **multimodal intelligence**: how models learn from, align, and generate information across modalities such as vision and language.

我的研究兴趣聚焦于 **多模态智能**：研究模型如何在视觉、语言等不同模态之间学习表示、建立对齐关系，并进行内容生成。

### Current interests / 当前兴趣

- **Multimodal learning / 多模态学习** — representation learning and cross-modal alignment.
- **Multimodal generation / 多模态生成** — controllable and coherent generation conditioned on multiple forms of information.
- **Computer vision / 计算机视觉** — visual representations and generative modeling as foundations for multimodal systems.
- **Research engineering / 科研工程** — reproducible environments, efficient experimentation, and reliable computing workflows.

## Research Approach / 研究方式

I am at an early stage of my PhD and currently emphasize three things:

1. **Build strong foundations** in machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision.
2. **Connect theory with implementation** through PyTorch experiments and careful validation.
3. **Document reproducible workflows** so that environments, experiments, and conclusions can be revisited.

博士初期，我会重点做好三件事：夯实机器学习与计算机视觉基础；通过 PyTorch 实践连接理论与实现；持续记录可复现的环境、实验和结论。
