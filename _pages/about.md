## English

I am a first-year PhD student in **Intelligent Science and Technology at Beijing Normal University (BNU)**. My research interests center on **multimodal intelligence**, with a current focus on learning and generation across visual and language modalities.

Before starting my doctoral study, I mainly worked on embedded development. That engineering background continues to shape how I approach research: understand the system, validate assumptions with experiments, and keep the workflow reproducible.

This website documents my research interests, technical practice, and selected learning notes. I am currently building a solid foundation in machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, PyTorch, and research computing for future multimodal research.

## 中文

我是 **北京师范大学智能科学与技术专业博士一年级学生**，昵称 **Shanshi**。目前的研究兴趣聚焦于 **多模态智能**，重点关注视觉与语言等模态之间的学习、理解与生成。

在博士阶段之前，我主要从事嵌入式开发。这段工程背景也影响着我的研究方式：先理解系统，再通过实验验证假设，并尽可能保证过程可复现。

这个主页用于整理我的研究方向、技术实践和阶段性学习笔记。目前我正在系统补充机器学习、深度学习、计算机视觉、PyTorch 与科研计算基础，为后续多模态研究建立可靠的知识与工程基础。
